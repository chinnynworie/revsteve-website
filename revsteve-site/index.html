About

A Life Devoted to the Word

Rev. Steve Obayojie serves as the Senior Pastor of Christ Victory Life Church International Headquarters, where he has dedicated his life to preaching the gospel with clarity, conviction, and compassion. Beyond the pulpit, he carries a deep burden for the health of the home — walking with couples and individuals as a Certified Marriage Counselor and Relationship Coach, helping them build marriages and relationships that reflect God's original design.

He holds a Diploma in Theology and a Post-Diploma in Theology, and continues to write and teach on faith, family, and purposeful living. He is married with three children, and considers the home his first and most sacred congregation.
